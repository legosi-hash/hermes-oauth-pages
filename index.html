Hermes AI Home Lab

Personal AI home-lab reporting integration

About this application

Hermes AI Home Lab is a privately operated home-lab project for managing AI development experiments, sandboxed testing, and operational reports. It uses Google Drive to transfer designated report files between systems controlled by the application owner.

This site describes the application's Google OAuth integration. It is not a public trading platform, a managed service, or a request for access to other people's Google Drive accounts.

Google Drive access

The integration requests limited Google Drive file access through the drive.file scope. Authorization is performed by the account owner. Google OAuth credentials are not embedded in this website or its public repository.

Read the Privacy Policy for details about data handling, security, and revoking access.
