Privacy Policy

Hermes AI Home Lab · Last updated: 18 November 2026

Purpose

Hermes AI Home Lab is a personal, self-hosted AI and automation laboratory. Its Google Drive connection is used to upload, download, and organize reports or files selected for the home-lab workflow.

Google user data accessed

The application requests the Google Drive OAuth scope drive.file, allowing access to files created by, or explicitly opened or shared with, the application within the scope's permissions. It does not request permission to read the account's entire Google Drive.

Use and sharing of data

Authorized Google Drive files are used to transfer home-lab reports, configuration-independent artifacts, and operational results between the account owner's systems. Data is not sold, used for advertising, or intentionally shared with third-party advertisers. Files are processed by Google Drive as part of the requested storage and transfer service.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials may be stored in the account owner's self-hosted system for authorized Drive operations. The deployment uses restricted local account permissions, but no system is completely immune to compromise. Report files may be stored locally and in the authorized Google Drive account; the account owner controls their retention and deletion.

Retention and deletion

The account owner can delete locally stored reports or Drive files and revoke the application's Google account access. Some operational logs or backups may remain until individually removed or rotated.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked from Google Account — Third-party connections. Revocation stops future authorized API access but does not automatically delete files already stored by the account owner.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner using the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
